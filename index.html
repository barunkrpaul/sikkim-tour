  2. Day 1 • Wed 18 November 2026 Arrival at NJP & transfer to Ravangla (South Sikkim) ▾

    Planned

    Transport: Early-morning arrival at NJP railway station, followed by private vehicle transfer towards Ravangla via scenic hill routes.

    Sightseeing: Buddha Park (Tathagata Tsal), featuring the 130-foot statue of Buddha.

    Overnight stay: Ravangla hotel / resort (name and contact to be announced).

  3. Day 2 • Thu 19 November 2026 Ravangla to Pelling (West Sikkim) via Namchi ▾

    Planned

    Morning: Proceed from Ravangla to Namchi. Visit Char Dham and Samdruptse Hill.

    Afternoon: Drive further into West Sikkim towards Pelling. Visit Pelling Skywalk and Rabdentse Ruins.

    Accommodation: not yet specified in the plan — faculty will announce.

  4. Day 3 • Fri 20 November 2026 Pelling exploration ▾

    Planned

    Sightseeing: Kanchenjunga Waterfall and sightseeing around Pelling.

  5. Day 4 • Sat 21 November 2026 Transfer to Falakata & return journey to Sealdah ▾

    Planned road transfer   Booked train

    If something goes wrong

    1. Stay calm and stay where you are if you are separated from the group. Call any faculty coordinator.
    2. For injury or illness call 108 (ambulance) or 112, then inform faculty.
    3. On the train, use Rail Madad 139 for medical help and 182 for security. Tell faculty your coach and berth.
    4. Keep your phone charged and carry your college ID and original photo ID at all times.
    5. Never go near cliff edges, rivers or waterfalls beyond safety barriers, and don't wander off alone.
