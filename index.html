Schedule & Sightseeing

Day-Wise Tour Itinerary

18–21 Nov 2026

Departure: Bandel Jn (BDC) at 18:58 hrs via 27575 Vande Bharat SL (Third AC / Coach B2)[cite: 2, 4].

Arrival: New Jalpaiguri Jn (NJP) at 01:40 hrs on 18 November 2026[cite: 2, 4].

Transfer: Early morning arrival at NJP railway station, followed by private vehicle transfer towards Ravangla via scenic hill routes.

Sightseeing: Visit the magnificent Buddha Park (Tathagata Tsal) featuring the 130-foot statue of Buddha.

Overnight Stay: Ravangla hotel / resort.

Morning: Proceed from Ravangla to Namchi. Visit Char Dham and Samdruptse Hill.

Afternoon: Drive further into West Sikkim towards Pelling. Visit Pelling Skywalk and Rabdentse Ruins.

20 Nov: Kanchenjunga waterfall sightseeing around Pelling.

21 Nov Departure: Transfer to Falakata for return journey on 13142 Teesta Torsha Express departing at 12:55 hrs (Coach S2)[cite: 2]. Arrival in Sealdah at 03:35 hrs on 22 Nov[cite: 2].
